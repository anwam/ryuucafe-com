# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Bangkok-based matcha drinkers browsing on mobile, mostly discovering RYUU via
Instagram/LINE before a first visit or delivery order. They're deciding in
seconds whether to tap through to an order (Line Man, Grab, Robinhood, Shopee
Food) or to visit the physical shop (Block Space 34, Senanikom). Bilingual
Thai/English audience — copy and type must serve both scripts natively, not
treat Thai as a translation afterthought.

## Product Purpose

Storefront for RYUU, a single-origin/ceremonial-grade matcha cafe in Bangkok.
The site's job is to communicate matcha quality (cultivar-level sourcing,
not generic "matcha latte" positioning) and get the visitor to either order
delivery or find the shop. Success = fast path from hero to an order action,
without diluting the craft/ceremonial impression.

## Brand Personality

Premium, ceremonial, refined. Slower-paced tea-ceremony feel over
fast-casual cafe energy — more like a tea house than a boba shop. Confidence
expressed through restraint (negative space, precise type, deliberate color)
rather than loud graphics or busy layouts. Japanese material culture as the
reference point: washi, deep matcha greens, gold/black ceremonial accents,
not "cute" or "kawaii."

## Anti-references

Not generic bubble-tea/fast-casual cafe design (bright rainbow menus, cartoon
mascots, heavy drop shadows). Not SaaS-template aesthetics (hero-metric
blocks, gradient text, generic rounded card grids repeated without
variation). Not tourist-trap "zen" cliché (bamboo textures, cherry blossom
stock photography, faux-calligraphy fonts).

## Design Principles

- Hierarchy through restraint: fewer competing visual signals per section,
  not more decoration.
- Craft over cliché: Japanese-inspired means considered typography, color,
  and spacing — not literal iconography (bamboo, sakura, torii gates).
- Order actions stay reachable: delivery/order CTAs should be visible near
  the point of decision (hero, header, per-product), not only in the footer.
- Bilingual by default: Thai and English type must both read cleanly at
  every scale used, since real content mixes scripts inline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No stated special requirements. Default to WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1
contrast, visible focus states on interactive elements (CTAs, menu,
carousel controls), reduced-motion alternative for hero animations/carousel
autoplay.
